    Embark on a transformative adventure with our summer camp course, 'Empower Your Leadership Journey,' exclusively designed for high school students.     Tech Pioneers:

    Tech Pioneers: Unleashing Adventures in AI, Game Design, Robotics, and VR is an exciting summer camp that blends robotics, AI, game design, and VR.     Psychology of Media:
    Influenced or Influencer?


    The 'Psychology of Media: Influence or Influencer?' track provides a hands-on learning experience that explores the relationship between psychology and media, marketing, and communication. Through interactive workshops and projects, participants will gain a comprehensive understanding of the psychology of media and its impact on society. They will be exposed to the latest theories and techniques in media psychology, while considering the impact of social media on mental health and well-being. Participants will also practice creating effective content that engages with audiences in ways that promote honest, positive social interactions. The 'Psychology of Media: Influence or Influencer?' track provides students with an enriching experience and the skills needed to navigate and succeed in today's media landscape.

FAQs

Are insurance, visa and travel expenses/arrangements included in the registration fees?
No, the registration fees cover accommodation, meals, excursion entrance fees, excursions� transport, modules, on-campus activities, and specific airport transfer trips. Participants are required to provide proof of insurance and arrange for their own travel and visa requirements if coming from outside the UAE.
Are students left unattended?
No, students are not left unattended unless sleeping in their own rooms. Even throughout the night, AUD provides continuous security and accommodation support unlock.
Is accommodation gender-segregated?
Student housing at AUD is strictly gender segregated. Males and females are not permitted nor able to mix with each other within student housing buildings and premises.
Are students allowed to roam around campus after 10:00 p.m.?
AUD has a strict curfew policy and students at the summer program are not allowed outside their rooms after 10:00 p.m.
Do students share rooms with each other?
Each student at the summer camp will have his/her own private bedroom and bathroom.
Is AUD culturally diverse?
AUD is very culturally diverse with students originating from over 100 countries around the world.
Is sleeping on campus mandatory or optional during the camp?
To ensure campers get the most out of the AUD Summer Camp, staying overnight on campus within the student housing facility for the entire duration of the camp is a mandatory requirement. This will provide a complete and enriching experience for everyone!
Is leaving the campus alone during the day allowed?
We understand the desire to leave campus, but you�ll have to be with the team at all times. We encourage you to make the most of the opportunities and activities available here on campus. There�s always something fun or interesting happening, so you won�t be missing out!
