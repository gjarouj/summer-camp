The AUD campus hosts over 30 student clubs and numerous annual events.

The university's student-to-faculty ratio is 15:1.

AUD offers exchange programs in the USA, Canada, Europe, and Asia.

93% of AUD's students are immediately placed in the job market upon graduation.

AUD's student body includes more than 105 nationalities.

The university offers over 15 undergraduate and graduate programs.

An academic advisor accompanies each student from the day they join until graduation.

Local, regional, and international employers highly seek AUD graduates.

AUD is the first American-accredited university in the GCC.

AUD Health Center offers services 24/7.

AUD's Athletic Division provides a developed program to complement the AUD academic experience.
